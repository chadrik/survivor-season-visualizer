Survivor dashboard mockup

The chosen design: the Transit Map layout with the player card in a side rail. Sample data is fictional: 3 seasons, a tribe swap, a 3-to-2 tribe shuffle, every exit type, and 4 returning players.

Open the dashboard →

Order defaults to Stable lanes. Switch to Longest-lasting first at the top; the choice is remembered in this browser.

Try

  • Click a blurred season, or flip “Reveal results”, then reload. The setting is remembered.
  • Hover a name. Its lines and its links to other seasons light up. Hover an exit or immunity icon for details.
  • Click a name to open the player card. Scroll through the seasons: the card follows the season you are reading.
  • Use the season index on the left to jump between seasons. Look at Season 3's “Watch first” list.
  • Narrow the window to see the phone layout: timelines scroll sideways and the card becomes a bottom sheet.
